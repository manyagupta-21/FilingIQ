# Loan & Credit Product Policy - Digital Bank 2024

## Home Loan

### Overview
The home loan product is designed for individual customers who wish to purchase, build, or renovate real estate. This is a long-term credit product with preferential interest rates and flexible procedures.

### Limits and Terms
Maximum loan amount: 70% of collateral value (LTV 70%). Loan amount ranges from 200,000,000 to 10,000,000,000 VND. Loan term from 5 to 25 years. Maximum principal grace period of 24 months.

### Home Loan Interest Rates
Years 1-2: fixed interest rate of 7.5%/year (promotional). From year 3 onwards: floating interest rate = Reference rate (12-month VNIBOR) + margin of 3.5%/year. Current estimated floating rate: 10.5-11%/year.

### Collateral
Houses, apartments, and residential land with red book/pink book ownership certificates. Properties involved in legal disputes are not accepted. Fire and explosion insurance must be purchased for the entire loan term.

### Home Loan Application Documents
Valid citizen ID/national ID card. Real estate sales contract/legal property documents. Income proof: payroll statement of the last 3-6 months or labor contract. Financial statements if the customer is self-employed. Processing time: 5-10 business days.

## SME Business Loan

### Target Customers
Small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) legally registered in Vietnam. Minimum operating period of 12 months. Most recent year's revenue of at least 1 billion VND.

### SME Loan Products
Working capital supplement loan: up to 5,000,000,000 VND, term up to 12 months, revolving. Fixed asset investment loan: up to 20,000,000,000 VND, term up to 7 years. Credit Line: flexible draw and repayment as needed.

### SME Eligibility and Interest Rates
Working capital loan interest rate: from 8.5%/year. Investment loan interest rate: from 9%/year. Appraisal fee: 0.1% of the loan value, minimum 1,000,000 VND. Collateral: real estate, inventory, or machinery and equipment.

## Credit Score & CIC

### What is the CIC Score
CIC (Credit Information Center) is the national credit information center managed by the State Bank of Vietnam. The CIC score reflects a customer's credit history from all credit institutions in Vietnam. The score ranges from 350-850, with higher scores indicating better loan eligibility.

### CIC Debt Classification
Group 1 (Standard debt): good, normal loan eligibility. Group 2 (Debt requiring attention): 10-90 days overdue, affects loan conditions. Group 3 (Substandard debt): 90-180 days overdue, restricted loan eligibility. Group 4 (Doubtful debt): 180-360 days overdue, very difficult to obtain loans. Group 5 (Potential loss debt): over 360 days overdue, unable to obtain loans.

### Improving Your Credit Score
Pay all loans and credit cards on time. Do not borrow from too many institutions simultaneously. Keep credit card utilization below 30%. A continuous good credit history of 12-24 months can significantly raise your score.
