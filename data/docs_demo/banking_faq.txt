# Digital Banking Product FAQ Document - Tyme Bank / GoTymeX

## 1. Payment Accounts

### Opening an Account
Customers can open a payment account entirely online via the mobile app. The process consists of 3 steps: download the app, verify identity with national ID/citizen ID card and a selfie photo, then set up a PIN. The whole process takes about 5-10 minutes. Customers do not need to visit a branch.

### Account Opening Requirements
Individual customers aged 18 or older, with a valid national ID/citizen ID card and an active Vietnamese phone number. No minimum balance is required to open an account. Foreign nationals legally residing in Vietnam can also open an account with a valid passport and visa.

### Minimum Balance
Basic payment accounts do not require a minimum balance. Term savings accounts require a minimum of 1,000,000 VND. Non-term savings accounts do not require a minimum balance.

---

## 2. Bank Cards

### Debit Cards
Domestic debit cards (Napas) are issued free of charge when opening an account. International Visa/Mastercard cards have an issuance fee of 50,000 VND. Domestic card transaction limit: 20,000,000 VND/day. International card limit: 50,000,000 VND/day.

### Credit Cards
Credit limits range from 5,000,000 to 200,000,000 VND depending on eligibility. Credit card interest rate: 2%/month (24%/year). Interest-free period up to 55 days. Annual fee: 200,000 VND/year, waived for the first year.

### Lock/Unlock Card
Customers can temporarily lock or unlock their cards directly within the mobile app 24/7 without contacting the call center. This feature helps protect the account immediately when a card is suspected of being lost.

---

## 3. Consumer Loans

### Cash Loans
Loan amounts range from 5,000,000 to 300,000,000 VND. Interest rates from 12%/year to 24%/year depending on the application and term. Loan terms from 6 to 60 months. Disbursement within 24 hours after approval. No collateral required for loans under 100,000,000 VND.

### Loan Eligibility
Customers aged 22 to 60. Minimum income of 5,000,000 VND/month. Good credit history (CIC). Currently residing and working in Vietnam.

### Loans for Phones / Electronic Devices
In partnership with retail partners (CellphoneS, The Gioi Di Dong, FPT Shop). Preferential interest rates from 0%/month for 3-6 month terms. In-store procedure: only citizen ID and phone number required, approval within 5 minutes.

---

## 4. Savings & Investment

### Term Savings
Terms from 1 to 36 months. Current interest rates (2024): 1 month 3.5%/year, 3 months 4.0%/year, 6 months 5.2%/year, 12 months 5.8%/year, 24 months 5.5%/year. Early withdrawal is allowed, with non-term interest rate applied.

### Non-Term Savings
Interest rate of 1.5%/year. Interest calculated daily, paid monthly. Withdraw anytime without fees.

### GoalSave (Goal-Based Savings)
A feature that helps customers set savings goals (buying a car, traveling, tuition fees...). Automatically deducts money periodically based on settings. Interest rate is 0.5%/year higher than regular savings. No penalty for early withdrawal if 80% of the goal is achieved.

---

## 5. Payments & Transfers

### Internal Transfers
Free 24/7. Instant, with no limit on the number of transactions.

### Interbank Transfers
Via Napas 247: free, instant, limited to 500,000,000 VND/transaction. Via SWIFT (international): fee of 200,000 - 500,000 VND/transaction, 2-5 business days.

### Bill Payments
Supports payment of electricity, water, internet, cable TV, insurance, and tuition fees at more than 2,000 partners. Schedule automatic monthly payments.

### QR Pay
Supports VietQR, payment at millions of points of sale nationwide. Integrated with e-wallets MoMo, ZaloPay, VNPay.

---

## 6. Security & Authentication

### Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
Mandatory for all financial transactions. Uses OTP via SMS or biometric authentication (fingerprint, Face ID).

### Risk-Based Transaction Limits
Transactions under 5,000,000 VND: PIN only. Transactions from 5,000,000 to 50,000,000 VND: PIN + OTP. Transactions over 50,000,000 VND: PIN + OTP + confirmation via registered email.

### Fraud Detection
AI system monitors transactions 24/7 in real time. Automatically blocks unusual transactions and notifies customers via push notification. Customers can confirm or report fraud directly in the app.

## 7. Customer Support

### Support Channels
Hotline: 1800 6X68 (free, 24/7). In-app live chat: 8:00 AM - 10:00 PM daily. Email: support@tymebank.vn. AI chatbot provides automated 24/7 support for frequently asked questions.

### Transaction Disputes
Dispute deadline: within 60 days of the transaction date. Processing time: 3-7 business days for domestic transactions, 15-45 days for international transactions. Provisional refund within 5 business days for valid disputes.

---

## 8. Compliance Regulations

### KYC (Know Your Customer)
Mandatory identity verification under Circular 09/2023/TT-NHNN. Periodic KYC information updates every 2 years. eKYC (electronic verification) is accepted for online account opening.

### eKYC Limits
eKYC tier 1 accounts: transaction limit of 100,000,000 VND/month. eKYC tier 2 accounts (enhanced verification): no limit. Upgrade by visiting a transaction point or completing video call verification.

### AML (Anti-Money Laundering)
Reporting of suspicious transactions in accordance with the Anti-Money Laundering Law. Cash transactions of 300,000,000 VND/day or more must be reported to the State Bank of Vietnam. Automatic monitoring and reporting in compliance with FATF regulations.
